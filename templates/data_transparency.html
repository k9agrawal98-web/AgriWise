AgriWise Trusted Data Policy

In strict adherence to academic and engineering integrity standards, AgriWise AI enforces zero fabrication of agronomic values, weather predictions, market spot prices, or model metrics. Where live third-party connectivity is unavailable, the system transparently indicates cached observations or unavailable states rather than generating synthetic approximations.
